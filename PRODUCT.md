## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: resellers in Mexico (mayoreo).** Small businesses and individuals who resell Korean skincare to their own customers. They buy in volume, compare unit prices and margins, pick between single units and boxes (`Presentacion`), and need to reach the order minimum (`CONFIG.MIN_ORDER_MXN`, currently $5,700 MXN) for Korea-imported items. Most browse and buy on their phones and are used to arranging purchases over WhatsApp.
- **Secondary: individual shoppers** who buy for their own routine, usually from the in-stock section (no minimum) or by meeting the minimum. The skin-type quiz and skin-concern tiles are aimed at them, and at resellers advising their own clients.
- **Operator: Mae**, who runs the business alone from `/admin.html`: she confirms transfer payments against uploaded receipts, edits orders, orders from the supplier, generates shipping labels, and notifies customers by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Alpacca is a catalog and checkout for Korean skincare (plus a separate "Cosmético Americano" collection of U.S. cosmetics) sold into Mexico. A buyer builds a cart, sees real product and shipping costs up front, and pays online by card (Mercado Pago) or by bank transfer with a receipt upload. The order then goes to Mae's admin panel for fulfillment. Success means resellers can put together a profitable order on their own, without negotiating over chat, and Mae can fulfill it with little manual work.

## Positioning

- **Direct import from Korea**: most of the catalog is ordered per unit straight from Korea. Pricing is formula-driven (cost × exchange rate + commission + U.S. tariff), so prices track real costs.
- **Pricing with room for resale margin**: wholesale-level prices, sold by single unit or by box.
- **In-stock in Mexico with no minimum**: a separate "En stock" section of ready-to-ship items, often heavily discounted, that doesn't count toward the order minimum.
- **Personal service**: a real person (Mae) answers on WhatsApp and follows up on every order.

## Operating Context

- The catalog, stock, prices, exchange rate, commission and shipping-rate tables all live in a Google Sheet (published CSV plus a private tab read through the Sheets API). Mae edits the Sheet and the site updates itself.
- Shipping has two legs: Korea→Mexico (weight-based rate table, USD) and national delivery via Estafeta Terrestre (zip-code zone × weight, MXN). Labels are generated by comparing quotes from Envíos Perros and Skydropx.
- Two fixed prices are published ahead of time: the transfer price (`Precio`) and the card price (`Precio Tarjeta`). Adding a card surcharge at checkout is not legal in Mexico, so a transfer discount is used instead.
- WhatsApp is the main channel for questions, order notices to Mae (Meta Business templates), customer confirmations and tracking numbers.
- Customer accounts ("Mi cuenta") match orders to accounts by phone number.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML + Tailwind (compiled `styles.css`) + vanilla JS (`app.js`, `index.html`, `admin.html`); Netlify Functions and Netlify Blobs on the server side. Domain: alpacca.mx.
- Sections driven by data (Best Seller, skin-type quiz, brands, in-stock, time-limited deal) hide themselves when the Sheet has no data for them. They never show placeholder or invented content.
- The Cosmético Americano collection has its own cart, a minimum order quantity per shade, its own order minimum ($18,000 MXN), and shipping quoted separately over WhatsApp.
- Terminology: *Carrito* is what the buyer builds before paying ("Tu carrito"); *pedido* is what exists once it's placed. Don't call the cart a *cotización*: prices are fixed and payment happens on the site ("cotizar" is kept only for shipping that is quoted separately, as in Cosmético Americano). Also *Precio por transferencia* / *con tarjeta*, *Presentación* (Pieza individual / Caja con N piezas), *En stock*, *Pedido mínimo*, *Agotado*, *Best Seller*, *TipoPiel*.
- The USD price toggle is for reference only; every charge is in MXN.
- There is deliberately no full "all products" grid with filters; buyers browse the curated sections and use search.

## Brand Commitments

- **Alpacca is the brand; Mae runs it.** Customers see Alpacca: the alpaca mascot and logos (`assets/logo-wordmark.png`, `assets/logo-full.png`, `assets/mascot.png`, favicons) and the brand typeface "Ready to Party" (`fonts/ReadyToParty.ttf`) for headings.
- Voice: Mexican Spanish, warm and informal (*tú*), practical and direct about prices, minimums and shipping. It uses some emoji, as the existing copy does.

## Evidence on Hand

- Brands carried include Anua, Dr. Althea, Centellian24, Medicube, Skin1004, mixsoon, Tocobo, COSRX, Beauty of Joseon, NIDA and others (whatever is in the Sheet).
- **No customer reviews or testimonials exist.** Don't fabricate them. Social links are currently empty.
- No payment-brand logo strip; Mercado Pago's own button branding is enough.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the real cost before commitment.** Product, shipping and transfer-vs-card prices appear before the buyer chooses how to pay, with no surprises at checkout.
2. **Serve the reseller's math.** Unit vs. box, minimums, stock counts and margins should be easy to scan and compare.
3. **The Sheet is the source of truth.** Anything data-driven hides when there's no data, and nothing on the site is invented.
4. **Keep the human thread.** Automation supports Mae's personal WhatsApp service; it doesn't replace it.
5. **Keep operations light for a one-person business.** Every admin feature should save Mae a manual step.
